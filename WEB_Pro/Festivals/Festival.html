Festivals of India

India is land of diverse culture and traditions. celebrated through many colorful festivals . Festivals like Diwali, Holi, Onam etc.bring people together with joy, delicious food, music, and traditional celebrations. They showcase india's rich heritage and spirit of unity

Diwali

The Festival of lights, celebrated with lamps, sweets , prayers and fireworks.

Holi

The festivals of colors, celebrated by playing with vibrant colors and enjoying time with loved ones.

Onam

A harvest festival of kerala, celebrated with flower decorations,traditional feasts,and cultural activities.
