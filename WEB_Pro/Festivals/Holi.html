HOLI

Holi is a major ancient Hindu spring festival, widely celebrated as the "Festival of Colours," "Festival of Love," and "Festival of Spring." It marks the arrival of spring, the end of winter, and the blossoming of love, while also serving as a festive day to meet others, play, laugh, forget and forgive, and repair broken relationships. The festival commemorates the divine love of Radha and Krishna and the ultimate triumph of good over evil, honoring the story of Prahlad and the burning of the demoness Holika. To celebrate, people light massive communal bonfires on the eve of the festival, known as Holika Dahan, and on the next day, they take to the streets for Rangwali Holi to playfully smear each other with vibrant colored powders (gulal), drench one another with water, dance to traditional music, and feast on delicacies like gujiya and thandai.
