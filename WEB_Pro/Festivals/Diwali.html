DIWALI

Diwali, also known as Deepavali, is the premier five-day Hindu festival of lights, symbolizing the spiritual victory of light over darkness, good over evil, and knowledge over ignorance. Celebrated globally between mid-October and mid-November based on the lunisolar calendar, the festival historically commemorates the triumphant return of Lord Rama, his wife Sita, and brother Lakshmana to their kingdom of Ayodhya after 14 years in exile and defeating the demon king Ravana. To welcome them, the citizens lit illuminated paths with rows of clay lamps (diyas), a tradition carried on today alongside vibrant floor patterns (rangoli). The multi-day festivities feature the cleaning and decorating of homes, dressing up in new festive attire, grand family feasts with an assortment of mithai (sweets), and prayers to Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth, for prosperity in the upcoming year.
